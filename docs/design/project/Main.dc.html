Spinfolk

One phone, a bottle, a circle of friends. Dark, calm room light. Two role colors do all the talking: teal takes, coral gives. Real native glass for controls; Flutter draws the game.

Color

Dark only for v1: party rooms are dim and a bright screen blinds the circle. Every role color is paired with a text label and a shape cue, never color alone. Contrast measured against bg.base.

Motion & haptics

Glass material

Glass lives only on native control islands (picker sheet, action bar, session button). Never on the dial or seat markers — those are Flutter canvas.
