Dial & bottle at every count

Seat 1 at twelve o’clock, clockwise. Neck (teal cap) always lands exactly on the taker. Base (coral cap) points to the giver — exact for even N, connector arc for odd N.

N = 2Taker P1 · Giver P2. Other player is always giver.
N = 5 · oddTaker P1 · Giver P3. Giver = seat 3 (one of two nearest-opposite). Dotted coral arc from base to giver. Bottle stays straight.
N = 8 · evenTaker P3 · Giver P7. Exact opposite: giver = (taker + 4) mod 8.
N = 60Taker P7 · Giver P37. Ticks only. Selected seats get long colored ticks and numbers; big labels live in the role panel.
Bottle anatomyNeutral frosted body. Teal lip = taker end. Coral base band = giver end. Artwork drawn neck-up (0 rad = twelve o’clock), so target rotation = θ(taker) with no offset.
ScalingDial = min(width, height − chrome) − 40. Bottle length 62% of radius. Marker size clamps 28–44 px; above 12 players switch to ticks so nothing overlaps on iPhone SE.
Honest limitAt 60 players the phone cannot be read by everyone. Speech announcement plus large role panel carry the result; the dial is confirmation.
